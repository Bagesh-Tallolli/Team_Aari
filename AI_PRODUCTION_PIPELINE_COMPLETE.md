### Label Generation Logic
```python
# WHO + ICMR Aligned Classification
if BMI < 15 AND calorie_ratio < 0.55:
    status = Underweight (high probability)
elif BMI 15-18.5 OR calorie_ratio 0.5-0.75:
    status = At Risk
else if BMI > 18.5 AND calorie_ratio > 0.75:
    status = Healthy
```
