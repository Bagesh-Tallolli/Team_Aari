7. CLINICAL & ENGINEERING INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Calorie & Protein Fulfillment Dominance:
   Together, `school_meal_calorie_fulfillment_pct` (26.6%) and 
   `school_meal_protein_fulfillment_pct` (25.3%) contribute over 51.8% of the
   model's decision boundaries. This directly validates that real-time IoT portion
   dispensation and hardware scale feedback provide the single strongest predictive
   signal for detecting child malnutrition risk.

2. Integration with Anthropometric WHO Z-Scores:
   The `pediatric_z_score_category` (17.1%) and child `bmi` (6.3%) ground the model
   in internationally recognized clinical pediatrics, ensuring classifications 
   distinguish between healthy children eating normal portions versus chronically 
   underweight children experiencing severe dietary deficits.

3. Role of 7-Day Longitudinal Moving Averages:
   `seven_day_avg_daily_calories` and `seven_day_avg_daily_protein` allow the 
   classifier to filter out isolated one-off missed meals and flag persistent,
   repeated low intake patterns across consecutive school days.

4. Assistive Screening Disclaimer:
   This machine learning pipeline operates as an assistive screening indicator for
   the Mid-Day Meal school feeding program. It is designed to alert teachers,
   parents, and administrative staff to potential nutritional gaps, and does NOT
   substitute for formal clinical examinations by a qualified pediatrician.
